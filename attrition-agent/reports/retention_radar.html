Guardrails built into the agent

  • Decision support only. The agent never stops a promotion, raise or training; a manager and HR decide.
  • Every flag shows its reasons in plain language, so it can be challenged.
  • Only HR records the company already holds. No personal social media or LinkedIn monitoring.
  • Access limited to the HR head and CEO; each run is saved with its inputs for audit.
  • Bias checks (below) rerun automatically every month on real outcomes.

Sheet 07

Data and method

Data this run used

To run on your data, fill in data_template/Retention_Radar_Data_Template.xlsx and run python run_agent.py --data <file>. Only employees, exits and compensation are required; every extra table sharpens the signals.

How it works

  • Every month, 29 signals are computed for each engineer from records dated before that month only.
  • Twelve models (one per horizon, 1–12 months) learn which signal patterns came before each type of resignation in past years.
  • Each flag is explained by resetting one signal group at a time to the company norm and measuring how much the risk falls.
  • Backtest: models trained only on data before the test date, then compared with what really happened.

Limits to keep in mind

  • A risk score is a probability, not a verdict; many High-risk people will stay.
  • Personal reasons (family, health, relocation) leave little trace in HR data and are hard to predict.
  • Competitor exits are clearer 1–3 months ahead than 9 months ahead.
  • Accuracy depends on history: aim for 2–3 years and 100+ past exits with reasons recorded.
